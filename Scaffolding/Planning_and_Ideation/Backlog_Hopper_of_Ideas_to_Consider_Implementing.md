# Backlog — Hopper of Ideas to Consider Implementing

A living log of ideas raised but deliberately **not** in the v1 scope (see
`Objective_of_project.md`). Nothing here is committed — it's a holding place so
good ideas aren't lost while we keep the first release tight.

Each entry is a user story (**As a … I want … so that …**) with the date it was
first raised and who suggested it.

| # | User story | Date first raised | Suggested by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Adding to the hopper

When a new idea surfaces, add a row with: the user story in **As a … I want … so
that …** form, the date it was first raised (`YYYY-MM-DD`), and who suggested it.
Keep entries as ideas — promotion into a real phase is a separate, deliberate
decision.

> **Still open:** there are other club problems worth capturing that weren't
> detailed in the planning conversation. As those get articulated, log them here
> as user stories rather than letting them expand the v1 build.
